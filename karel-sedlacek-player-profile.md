- **Televised Craze on Nova Sport:** Broadcaster Nova Sport acquired exclusive multi-year rights to PDC tournaments. Boosted by Sedláček’s weekly performances and the rise of youth star Adam Gawlas, viewing figures multiplied by over 400%.

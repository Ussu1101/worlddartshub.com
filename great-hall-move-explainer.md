# Ally Pally Great Hall Move (2026/27): What Attending Fans Must Know

For almost two decades, the West Hall of Alexandra Palace served as the spiritual cathedral of world darts. Since the PDC moved its marquee event from the Circus Tavern in Purfleet in 2007/08, the intimate, cavernous, and famously raucous atmosphere of the West Hall became legendary in global sport.

Beginning with the **2026/27 PDC World Darts Championship**, the tournament embarks on a transformative new era by shifting entirely into Alexandra Palace’s **Great Hall**. Secured by an exclusive five-year agreement between the PDC and Alexandra Palace Trust running through at least 2031, this move represents the most substantial expansion in the championship's history.

Whether you are a seasoned Ally Pally veteran or planning your maiden voyage, here is everything you need to know about the Great Hall transition.

---

## 1. Why Is the World Darts Championship Moving?

The decision to relocate across the palace complex was driven by unyielding global demand. In recent years, ticket ballots have been overwhelmed, with hundreds of thousands of registered fans failing to secure entry for peak sessions.

### Key Expansion Metrics: West Hall vs. Great Hall

| Dimension / Metric | Traditional West Hall (2007–2025) | Expanded Great Hall (2026/27 Onwards) | Operational Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Capacity per Session** | ~3,200 spectators | 5,000+ spectators | Substantial increase in seats per session |
| **Total Tournament Footfall** | ~90,000 across 28 sessions | ~180,000 across 28 sessions | Historic attendance record for global darts |
| **Auditorium Scale** | Compact Victorian hall | Expansive Victorian exhibition hall | Greatly increased seating footprint |
| **Ceiling Clearance** | Lower overhead clearance | High barrel-vaulted ceiling | Accommodates suspended 360° LED screen arrays |
| **Acoustic Profile** | Intense, reverberant box acoustic | Grand arena acoustic with tuned line-arrays | Superior chant projection and crowd roar |
| **Contract Security** | Rolling lease agreements | Fixed agreement through at least 2031 | 5-year guarantee at Alexandra Palace |

## 2. Floor Layout, Sightlines & The New Seating Architecture

Moving to the Great Hall presents both opportunities and challenges for spectator sightlines. The physical regulation dartboard measures just 45 cm in diameter with a 50-point bullseye measuring a mere 1.27 cm wide. Beyond the first few table rows, spectators rely on video screens to track individual darts.

To address this, the Great Hall production features state-of-the-art technological enhancements:

### Suspended High-Definition LED Cube & Ring Truss
Above the central table section, the PDC and Sky Sports have installed a 360-degree, high-resolution suspended LED cube complemented by flanking perimeter display banners. Ultra-low-latency broadcast feeds ensure zero lag between the dart striking the sisal and the board visual updating.

### Elevated Staging & Steeper Grandstands
The main walk-on stage and oche have been purpose-built for the larger space, complemented by tiered bleacher grandstands flanking the hall built at a steeper rake to provide clear sightlines above floor table level.

### Expanded Table Blocks
Floor tables remain the most sought-after tickets in darts. In the Great Hall, floor tables accommodate up to 10 guests per trestle. Wider central and transverse aisles have been engineered to improve crowd circulation during walk-ons and intervals.

## 3. Bars, Catering & Concourse Flow: Managing 5,000 Fans

One of the greatest operational friction points in the West Hall was bar congestion and restroom queuing between matches. The Great Hall layout expands hospitality infrastructure:

- **Extended Flank Bars:** Long continuous draught beer bars run along the outer perimeter walls to handle intermission surges.
- **4-Pint Pitcher Service:** To minimize transaction frequency, main bars feature reusable 4-pint pitcher dispensing alongside standard 2-pint and 1-pint options.
- **Dedicated Village & Food Court:** The adjacent Palm Court continues as the primary entry foyer and street food market, housing gourmet burger vendors, loaded fries, German bratwurst stations, and vegetarian/vegan food trucks.
- **Auxiliary Restrooms:** Purpose-built auxiliary restroom blocks are positioned behind the tiered grandstands to reduce queuing times.

## 4. Arrival, Security & Public Transport Updates

With over 5,000 spectators arriving simultaneously for evening sessions, fans must plan their travel and security arrival carefully:

1. **Palm Court Entrance:** All ticket holders must enter through the iconic Palm Court entrance on the south side of Alexandra Palace.
2. **High-Speed Optical Scanners:** Only official digital barcodes in the SeeTickets app are accepted. Screenshots or printed PDFs from secondary resellers will be rejected at the turnstiles.
3. **Transport Connections:**
   - **Wood Green (Piccadilly Line):** Connect to the **W3 bus**, which drops fans directly at the Palm Court gates.
   - **Alexandra Palace Station (Great Northern / Thameslink):** 10-minute walk through Alexandra Park or via event shuttle buses.
   - **Departure Queues:** Post-event W3 bus queues can be long. Many veteran fans prefer walking down the hill to Wood Green Station or pre-booking licensed taxis from the designated rank.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is the World Darts Championship moving to the Great Hall?
The tournament is moving from Alexandra Palace's West Hall to the Great Hall due to unprecedented global ticket demand. The Great Hall increases per-session capacity from approximately 3,200 to over 5,000 spectators, lifting total tournament attendance past 180,000 across the three-week event.

### How long will the darts remain in the Great Hall at Alexandra Palace?
The Professional Darts Corporation (PDC) and Alexandra Palace signed an exclusive five-year agreement securing the Great Hall as the host venue of the World Darts Championship through to at least 2031.

### Will the move make it easier to see the dartboard?
The physical dartboard remains regulation size (45 cm), but the Great Hall installation features an expanded matrix of ultra-high-definition ceiling-hung and stage-side LED screens, ensuring all 5,000 spectators have direct, unobstructed digital sightlines of every thrown dart.

### Does the Great Hall move mean more table tickets or tiered tickets?
Both ticket categories increase in volume, but the largest proportion of new capacity comes via expanded, steeper raked tiered grandstands flanking the hall, giving more fans clear sightlines above floor table level.

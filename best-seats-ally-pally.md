## 2. The Great Hall Era (2026/27 Onwards): What It Means for Seating

For decades, the PDC World Darts Championship was hosted in Alexandra Palace’s West Hall, accommodating approximately 3,200 spectators per session. Starting with the 2026/27 tournament, the event moves permanently into the magnificent **Great Hall** under a partnership running until at least 2031.

This historic move increases individual session capacity to over **5,000 spectators** and lifts total tournament attendance past 180,000 fans across the festive period. Key changes include:
- **More Tiered Options:** Larger, steeper temporary grandstand structures provide steeper rakes and clearer sightlines over the floor tables.
- **Enhanced Big-Screen Grid:** An expanded matrix of ultra-high-definition ceiling-hung and stage-side LED screens covers the 4,000-square-meter hall.
- **Dedicated Acoustic Zones:** The cavernous Victorian hall retains sound exceptionally well, amplifying the roar of 5,000 supporters.

## 3. Floor Table Seating: The Epicentre of the Ally Pally Party

- **Bench Configuration:** Tables are long wooden banquet trestles, usually seating six people per table. Smaller groups share tables with fellow darts fans.
- **Player Catwalk Proximity:** Tables directly flank the raised central walkway, putting fans inches away from walk-ons.
- **The Fancy Dress Heartbeat:** Floor tables are where the majority of elaborate group fancy dress costumes congregate.
- **Sightline Reality:** Because a dartboard is only 45 cm in diameter and the floor is flat, table spectators watch approximately 80% of active play on the overhead LED screens rather than tracking physical darts with the naked eye.

---

## 4. Tiered Grandstand Seating: The Purist's Choice

- **Uninterrupted Elevation:** Stepped seating guarantees an unobstructed view over standing floor fans.
- **Straight-Ahead Screen Viewing:** Screens are positioned at eye level, eliminating neck strain.
- **Individual Assigned Seats:** Standard tip-up arena seats provide defined personal space and back support over a four-hour session.
- **Effortless Concourse Exits:** Wide aisles lead straight to concourses, bars, food vendors, and restrooms.
- **Designated Family Section:** Block D traditionally offers an alcohol-free, family-oriented environment for younger fans and families.

## 5. Session Differences: Afternoon vs Evening Atmosphere

- **Afternoon Sessions (12:30 GMT):** More relaxed, social pace with shorter queues and higher family attendance. Ideal for darts purists wanting to follow every leg closely.
- **Evening Sessions (19:00 GMT):** High-energy, loud, party-centric atmosphere dominated by fancy dress, singing, and full beer runs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are table seats or tiered seats better at Alexandra Palace?
Neither is universally better; it depends on your priority. Table seats offer the authentic, high-energy party atmosphere and close proximity to the stage and player walk-on catwalk. Tiered seats offer elevated, unobstructed sightlines looking over the crowd directly at the giant screens, with faster access to bars and restrooms.

### Can you see the dartboard from table seats at Ally Pally?
No spectator beyond the first two or three rows of tables can clearly see the physical dartboard wires with the naked eye. Because the board is only 45 cm in diameter and over 15 to 50 meters away, table spectators watch the live scoring and doubles via giant high-definition LED screens flanking the stage.

### How many people sit at a table at Alexandra Palace darts?
Floor tables at Alexandra Palace typically seat six people per trestle banquet table. Tickets are sold individually or as whole tables, meaning smaller parties are seated alongside fellow darts supporters.

### Which seats are best for families and first-time visitors?
Tiered grandstand seating—particularly the designated Family Section (typically Block D in previous configurations)—is strongly recommended for families, younger fans, and first-time spectators wanting a clearer view without being squeezed into banquet benches amidst celebratory beer runs.

### What is the difference between afternoon and evening sessions?
Afternoon sessions (starting at 12:30 GMT) feature a slightly more relaxed, darts-focused crowd with easier bar queues and family attendance. Evening sessions (starting at 19:00 GMT) are louder, heavily dominated by fancy dress, nonstop chants, and a vibrant party atmosphere.

### Where can I safely buy official tickets for Ally Pally darts?
Official tickets must be purchased through See Tickets via the PDC priority sales window for PDCTV Annual Members, followed by the general public sale. Official face-value resale is exclusively operated via See Tickets Fan-to-Fan Marketplace. Avoid unauthorised secondary ticket websites where tickets risk cancellation.

## 1. The Core Distinction: Official Prize Money vs Sponsor Bonuses

Under official Professional Darts Corporation (PDC) tournament rules, there is **no guaranteed baseline prize money** for throwing a nine-dart finish. Tournament prize funds are distributed purely by round progression. While past decades featured rolling £5,000 or £25,000 tournament bonuses, the PDC officially phased out standing nine-dart funds in 2019 to reinvest those funds directly into elevated round-by-round prize money across all tour players.

Any substantial cash prize a player receives today is almost exclusively funded by **commercial title sponsors** (e.g. Paddy Power, BetMGM, William Hill). These are bespoke marketing promotions negotiated on a per-tournament basis.

## 2. The Landmark £180,000 Bonus: Paddy Power's "The BIGGER 180"

Title sponsor Paddy Power introduced "The BIGGER 180" campaign at the PDC World Darts Championship at Alexandra Palace. Under this promotion, every televised nine-dart finish triggered a **£180,000 total bonus pool** split three ways (£60,000 each):

1. **£60,000 to the Player:** Performance cash bonus to the thrower.
2. **£60,000 to Charity:** Donated directly to Prostate Cancer UK.
3. **£60,000 to a Fan:** Handed to a randomly selected ticket-holding spectator attending that session at Ally Pally.

### Verified Historic Payouts (2024/25 World Darts Championship):
- **Christian Kist (17 December 2024):** Hit a 141 checkout (T20, T19, D12) in Round 1 against Madars Razma. Kist received £60,000, Prostate Cancer UK received £60,000, and a spectator named Kris from Sutton won £60,000. (Kist lost the match 3–1).
- **Damon Heta (27 December 2024):** Hit another 141 checkout in Round 3 against Luke Woodhouse. Heta collected £60,000, charity received £60,000, and fan Mauro took home £60,000. (Heta lost the match 4–3).
- **Total Paid Out by Paddy Power:** £360,000 across the tournament.

## 4. Floor Events vs Televised Majors

- **Televised Stages (Sky Sports / ITV / DAZN):** High commercial exposure leads sponsors to create promotional bonuses (such as Paddy Power's £180k pool).
- **ProTour Floor (Players Championships & Q-School):** £0 direct cash bonus. Players receive a commemorative gold lapel badge and official statistical logging on DartConnect.
- **Order of Merit Status:** Sponsor bonuses and nine-dart prizes are non-ranking funds and never count toward the official two-year rolling PDC Order of Merit.

---

## 5. What Happens When Multiple Nine-Darters Are Thrown?

In traditional PDC tournaments with a fixed prize pool (e.g. £25,000), multiple nine-darters in the same event resulted in the prize pool being shared equally among the players. Under modern insured commercial promotions (such as Paddy Power in 2024/25), each nine-darter is treated as a separate qualifying event, paying out the full bonus to each player and partner.

## 6. Sporting Significance vs Financial Reward

A nine-dart finish is an extraordinary athletic feat, but it wins only a single leg. Both Christian Kist and Damon Heta lost their matches immediately after throwing their nine-darters at Ally Pally. In contrast, winning the World Championship title earns £1,000,000 from the £5,000,000 total prize fund and secures top world ranking.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do PDC darts players automatically get prize money for hitting a nine-dart finish?
No. There is no standard, guaranteed baseline prize money for hitting a nine-dart finish in the official PDC rulebook. Payouts depend entirely on whether an event's title sponsor funds an active promotional bonus or if a designated tournament rollover fund is in effect.

### What was the Paddy Power £180,000 nine-dart bonus?
During the Paddy Power World Darts Championship, title sponsor Paddy Power created 'The BIGGER 180' campaign, offering a £180,000 bonus pool per nine-darter split three ways: £60,000 to the throwing player, £60,000 to charity partner Prostate Cancer UK, and £60,000 to a randomly selected fan in the Alexandra Palace arena.

### Which players triggered Paddy Power's £60k x 3 nine-dart payout?
At the 2024/25 World Darts Championship, two players triggered the payout: Christian Kist on 17 December 2024 against Madars Razma, and Damon Heta on 27 December 2024 against Luke Woodhouse. On each occasion, Paddy Power paid £60,000 to the player, £60,000 to Prostate Cancer UK, and £60,000 to a lucky spectator in the crowd.

### What is the biggest nine-dart bonus ever won?
The largest individual cash prize ever won for a single televised nine-dart finish belongs to John Lowe, who earned £102,000 at the MFI World Matchplay on 13 October 1984. By comparison, the tournament winner's prize that year was just £12,000.

### Do players get bonus money for nine-darters on the ProTour floor?
No. On the non-televised PDC ProTour (Players Championship events), players receive no official cash bonus for hitting a nine-dart finish. They receive commemorative pin badges and official statistical recognition, but zero financial bonus.

### Does nine-dart finish bonus money count towards the PDC Order of Merit?
No. Any commercial sponsor bonus or special nine-dart prize money is strictly classified as unranked bonus money and does not count towards the official two-year rolling PDC Order of Merit world rankings.

## Executive Summary
Staged weekly from a bespoke broadcast arena in Portsmouth, the MODUS Super Series has become the premier proving ground in world darts. This guide explains how the weekly tournament structure works, the £25,000 Champions Week finale, free global broadcast channels, and the platform that nurtured Luke Littler.

---

## 1. What Is the MODUS Super Series?

The **MODUS Super Series** is an innovative, weekly made-for-broadcast darts tournament organized by MODUS Events in partnership with the Live League. Broadcast live from a purpose-built studio arena known as the "Live Lounge" in Portsmouth, England, the competition runs virtually all year round, delivering over 30 hours of live action every single week.

---

## 2. Eligibility: Who Can Compete?

- **Strict Non-Tour Card Rule:** Open strictly to players who do **NOT** hold a current two-year PDC Tour Card.
- **Competitor Pool:** Emerging youth stars, former major champions who lost their Tour Cards (e.g. Colin Osborne, Robert Thornton), international qualifiers, and top independent players.
- **Tour Card Graduation:** Winning a Tour Card at PDC Q-School immediately renders a player ineligible for future MODUS Super Series competition.

---

## 3. The Weekly Tournament Format: Monday to Saturday

Twelve players compete each week across three preliminary groups:

- **Group A (Mon–Wed morning, 09:30 GMT):** 6 players, round-robin. 1st place advances directly to Saturday Finals Night. 2nd–6th drop to Groups B & C.
- **Group B (Thu–Fri late night, 22:00 GMT):** 6 players. Top 3 advance to Saturday Finals Night; bottom 3 eliminated.
- **Group C (Thu–Fri afternoon, 13:00 GMT):** 6 players. Top 2 advance to Saturday Finals Night; bottom 4 eliminated.
- **Finals Night (Saturday, 19:30 GMT):** 6 Qualifiers divided into two groups of 3, followed by semi-finals and the Grand Final. Winner takes £5,000 and qualifies for Champions Week.

---

## 4. The Pinnacle: Champions Week

Every 13th week of the series is **Champions Week**, bringing together the 12 weekly winners from the preceding 12 weeks. The winner of Champions Week collects a **£25,000 top prize**, the Champions Trophy, and elite recognition across the global darts fraternity.

---

## 5. Prize Money Breakdown

The circuit awards over **£1,000,000 in total prize money** annually:
- **Weekly Winners (Weeks 1–12):** £5,000.
- **Champions Week Winner (Week 13):** £25,000.
- **Field Distribution:** Prize money awarded down the field to all participants based on finishing positions.

- **Official YouTube Channel:** Complete free live 1080p global streaming of every session (Mon–Sat) on the official MODUS Super Series YouTube channel, with full archives and highlights.

## 7. Famous Alumni: The Luke Littler Proving Ground

- **Luke Littler:** Won back-to-back Champions Week titles in Series 3 (May 2023, £20,000) and Series 4 (August 2023, £20,000) as a 16-year-old, mastering stage pressure prior to his historic 2024 World Championship breakthrough.

## 8. Role in the Darts Ecosystem: Pathway vs Independent Tour

- Not an official PDC-sanctioned pathway (does not award Tour Cards or Order of Merit ranking points).
- High-intensity stage practice under professional cameras and stage lighting.
- The ultimate preparatory ground for annual PDC Q-School.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the MODUS Super Series?
The MODUS Super Series is a weekly, made-for-television darts competition staged in a purpose-built arena in Portsmouth, England. It features top non-PDC Tour Card holders, emerging prospects, international qualifiers, and established legends competing across Monday to Saturday.

### Who is eligible to play in the MODUS Super Series?
The competition is strictly open to players who do NOT currently hold a full PDC Tour Card. If a player wins a PDC Tour Card at Q-School, they immediately become ineligible to compete in future MODUS Super Series events.

### How does the weekly MODUS Super Series format work?
Each week features 12 players divided into three groups: Group A plays Monday to Wednesday morning (winner advances directly to Saturday). Groups B and C play Thursday and Friday (top 3 from Group B and top 2 from Group C advance). The six qualifiers contest Finals Night on Saturday to crown the Weekly Champion.

### What is Champions Week and how much prize money is awarded?
Every 13th week is Champions Week, bringing together the 12 weekly winners from the preceding 12 weeks of competition. The winner of Champions Week collects a £25,000 top prize and the series trophy, contributing to an annual prize fund exceeding £1,000,000.

### Where can I watch the MODUS Super Series live?
The MODUS Super Series is broadcast live globally for free on the official MODUS Super Series YouTube channel, as well as on Pluto TV via its dedicated darts streaming channel. Matches stream Monday through Saturday, covering more than 30 hours of live action weekly.

### Did Luke Littler play in the MODUS Super Series?
Yes. Luke Littler rose to prominence in the MODUS Super Series, winning back-to-back Champions Week titles in Series 3 (May 2023) and Series 4 (August 2023) as a 16-year-old before his historic run to the 2024 World Darts Championship final.

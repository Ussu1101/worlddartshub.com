# PDC Darts Calendar 2027: Comprehensive Season Guide

From the intense pressure of Qualifying School in early January to the winter coronation at Alexandra Palace, the **2027 PDC calendar** features 11 televised major championships, 14 continent-wide European Tour showcases, 30 ProTour floor tournaments, and expanding secondary circuits.

This comprehensive guide delivers the definitive timeline of dates, host venues, ranking implications, and broadcast channels for the entire 2027 professional season.

## 2. The 2027 PDC European Tour: 14 Continental Showcases

The European Tour represents one of the PDC's greatest commercial growth stories. Staged across three-day weekend festivals, each European Tour event features 48 players competing for £175,000 in total prize money (£30,000 to the champion), broadcast live on PDCTV and DAZN.

### 2027 Host Venues & Key Stops:
- **Germany (6 Stops):** Munich (German Darts Grand Prix over Easter weekend), Sindelfingen (European Darts Grand Prix), Riesa (International Darts Open), Hildesheim, Kiel, and Leverkusen.
- **Austria (2 Stops):** Graz (Austrian Darts Open) and Schwechat/Vienna.
- **Belgium (2 Stops):** Wieze (Belgian Darts Open) and Antwerp (Flanders Darts Trophy).
- **Central & Western Europe (4 Stops):** Budapest (Hungarian Darts Trophy), Prague (Czech Darts Open), Basel (Swiss Darts Trophy), and Leeuwarden (Dutch Darts Championship).

Crucially, European Tour prize money counts toward both the primary PDC Order of Merit and the **European Tour Order of Merit**, which dictates the 32-player field for October's European Championship in Dortmund.

## 3. The ProTour Engine Room: Players Championship Series

Away from live television cameras, the foundation of a professional darts career is forged on the ProTour. The 2027 series features **30 Players Championship events**:
- **Field:** All 128 PDC Tour Card holders, plus top-up reserve players from the Challenge Tour Order of Merit.
- **Structure:** 16 simultaneous dartboards played down to a champion in a single day.
- **Prize Fund:** £125,000 per event (£15,000 to the winner).
- **Host Centers:** Robin Park Tennis Centre (Wigan), Mattioli Arena (Leicester), and Halle 39 (Hildesheim).
- **Qualification Pathway:** Earnings determine the ProTour Order of Merit, which awards spots for the World Matchplay, World Grand Prix, Players Championship Finals, and World Darts Championship.

## 4. Secondary Circuits: Pathways to Stardom

The PDC ecosystem provides clear, transparent meritocratic progression across all playing demographics:

### Winmau Challenge Tour
For players who attended Q-School but failed to win a Tour Card. 24 events offering £15,000 per tournament. The top two players on the final Challenge Tour Order of Merit receive two-year PDC Tour Cards and automatic World Championship qualification.

### Winmau Development Tour
Dedicated to aspiring players aged 16 to 23. 24 events across the year. The top two players receive PDC Tour Cards and Alexandra Palace berths.

### PDC Women’s Series
24 standalone events staged across six weekends. Offers qualification spots for the Women's World Matchplay in Blackpool, the Grand Slam of Darts, and three places at the Alexandra Palace World Championship.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### When does the 2027 PDC darts season officially begin?
The 2027 season kicks off in early January with Qualifying School (Q-School) held simultaneously in Milton Keynes (UK) and Kalkar (Europe), followed immediately by the opening Players Championship floor events and The Masters.

### Which PDC tournaments in 2027 are ranked for the Order of Merit?
All 30 Players Championship events, the 14 European Tour tournaments, UK Open, World Matchplay, World Grand Prix, European Championship, Grand Slam of Darts, Players Championship Finals, and World Darts Championship count towards the two-year rolling PDC Order of Merit. The Premier League, World Cup of Darts, and Masters are unranked invitationals.

### Where will the 2027 World Matchplay be held?
The 2027 World Matchplay will take place in July at its iconic spiritual home, the Empress Ballroom at the Winter Gardens in Blackpool, England.

### How many European Tour events are scheduled for 2027?
The PDC European Tour features 14 scheduled three-day events spanning Germany, Austria, Belgium, the Netherlands, Hungary, Switzerland, and the Czech Republic, offering vital ranking prize money and European Championship qualification.

### When will the 2027/28 PDC World Darts Championship begin?
The 2027/28 PDC World Darts Championship will commence in mid-December 2027 and conclude with the final in early January 2028 at Alexandra Palace's Great Hall in London.

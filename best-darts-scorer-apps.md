# Best Darts Scorer Apps: Comprehensive Review & Comparison

*Last updated: October 2026 | Independently reviewed across iOS, Android, and web-based platforms*

Tossing darts at a board without tracking your numbers is fun, but if you want to improve your game, a dedicated **darts scorer app** is indispensable. 

Modern scoring apps do far more than eliminate manual arithmetic. They track your [three-dart average](/darts/average-explained/), calculate your First-9 scoring power, log your [checkout percentages](/players/checkout-percentage-explained/), simulate pressure through intelligent bot opponents, and connect you with remote players across the globe via live video.

However, the App Store and Google Play are flooded with dozens of generic apps—many cluttered with intrusive pop-up ads, clunky interfaces, or paywalled essentials. 

To help you choose the right companion for your tablet or phone, we tested the five leading darts scorer apps across hundreds of practice legs and league matches.

## 1. DartConnect: The Professional Gold Standard

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     DARTCONNECT SNAPSHOT                    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
|  Platform:    Web App (Runs on any iPad, Tablet, or PC)     |
|  Official:    Official Scoring Engine of the PDC ProTour    |
|  Strengths:   Professional legitimacy, DCTV live score feeds|
|  Best For:    Organised leagues, tournaments, super leagues |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Why It Leads the Field
DartConnect is the undisputed heavyweight of professional darts scoring. It is the official scoring system commissioned by the **Professional Darts Corporation (PDC)** to power every Players Championship floor event, the Winmau Challenge Tour, and the European Tour. 

Unlike traditional mobile apps downloaded from an app store, DartConnect runs directly in any modern web browser. This architectural choice makes it exceptionally stable on tablets mounted next to a home board or pub oche.

### Key Features
- **Dart-by-Dart Precision:** Rather than just entering your three-dart total (e.g. "60"), DartConnect allows stroke-by-stroke entry (e.g. Single 20, Single 20, Single 20), logging exact double attempts and misses.
- **DCTV Broadcast Integration:** League matches and tournament brackets can be broadcast live to the web, allowing friends, family, or spectators to track every dart in real time.
- **Deep Statistical Vault:** Premium members access lifetime historical records, tracking first-9 averages, checkout percentages by specific double ring, and head-to-head records.

### Limitations
The user interface prioritises data integrity and tournament efficiency over flashy graphics. It has a steeper learning curve for complete beginners who just want a quick score counter.

## 2. Target Darts Counter: Best for Online Multiplayer

Backed by equipment powerhouse Target Darts, **Target Darts Counter** (formerly known simply as *Darts Counter*) is arguably the sleekest mobile scoring application on the market.

### Key Features
- **Integrated Video Play:** The app's standout feature is its peer-to-peer camera system. Mount your phone or tablet pointing at the board, and you can play live matches against players anywhere in the world with real-time video verification.
- **Intelligent Bot System:** Offers adjustable virtual opponents ranging from beginner (30 average) to world-class tour standard (100+ average) with realistic scoring variance.
- **Voice Calling:** Includes crisp audio announcements that call out scores and remaining finishes.

### Best Used For
Casual players and competitive amateurs who cannot attend local leagues but want the adrenaline of playing real opponents from home.

## 3. My Dart Training: Best for Solo Practice & Drills

Created by developer Markus Fisch, **My Dart Training** is revered among competitive amateur dart players as the absolute benchmark for deliberate, structured skill building.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
|               FEATURED DRILLS IN MY DART TRAINING               |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
|  • Bob's 27:        Classic double-accuracy survival test       |
|  • Catch 40:        Pressure outshot routine from 61 to 100     |
|  • 121:             Three-visit finishing simulation            |
|  • Around the Clock: Singles, doubles, and trebles navigation   |
|  • Halve-It:        Target precision under penalty deduction    |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Why Practice Purists Love It
While other apps focus on playing 501 against friends, My Dart Training is built for the solo grinder. It incorporates every established [darts practice routine](/darts/practice-routines/) into automated, tracked routines:
- **Zero Internet Required:** The app works completely offline with zero latency.
- **Progress Tracking Graphs:** Generates clean trend lines displaying your scoring average and double conversion over days, weeks, and months.
- **Uncluttered & Lightweight:** Fast, responsive, and completely devoid of invasive video ads that disrupt practice rhythm.

## 4. Russ Bray Darts Scorer Pro: Best for Stage Atmosphere

If you miss the iconic, gravelly roar of legendary referee Russ Bray calling matches on television, **Russ Bray Darts Scorer Pro** brings the atmosphere of Alexandra Palace into your home.

### Key Features
- **Authentic Voice Samples:** Every score from 1 to 180 is voiced personally by Russ Bray, recorded in high-fidelity audio. Hitting a 180 triggers the unmistakable, bone-shaking roar that made Bray a sporting icon.
- **Solo & Multiplayer Modes:** Supports standard 501, 301, and Cricket play with multi-leg and multi-set tournament configurations.
- **No Monthly Subscription:** Sold as an affordable one-off download rather than a recurring monthly subscription.

### Limitations
While the audio experience is unmatched, the analytical depth and practice routines are more basic than those in My Dart Training or DartConnect.

## 5. Darts Mind: Best for Minimalist Speed

For players who find feature-heavy apps distracting, **Darts Mind** offers a beautifully designed, minimalist interface.

- **Fast Input:** Big, clean number pads allow you to enter scores in under a second.
- **Multiple Game Modes:** Flawless support for 501, 301, Cricket, Around the Clock, and Bermuda Triangle.
- **Apple Watch Integration:** Allows players to glance at remaining scores and calculate finishes directly from their wrist.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What app is used by the PDC for scoring?
The PDC uses **DartConnect** as its official scoring platform for all ProTour Players Championship events, the European Tour, Challenge Tour, Development Tour, and Qualifying School. Officials enter scores dart-by-dart on tablets, broadcasting real-time scores to fans via DartConnect TV (DCTV).

### Can you use darts scorer apps without an internet connection?
Yes. Apps such as **My Dart Training**, **Russ Bray Darts Scorer Pro**, and **Darts Mind** operate 100% offline with no Wi-Fi or mobile data required. **Target Darts Counter** also supports offline play against bots. Only online multiplayer features and real-time live broadcasting (like DartConnect TV) require an active internet connection.

### What is the best free darts practice app?
**My Dart Training** is widely regarded as the best free practice app. It includes comprehensive solo training routines—such as Bob's 27, Catch 40, 121, and Around the Clock—and full historical stat tracking with minimal non-intrusive advertisements.

### Do darts scorer apps calculate remaining checkouts automatically?
Yes. All top scorer apps automatically display recommended two-dart and three-dart outshot combinations whenever your score falls to 170 or below. They dynamically update recommendations if you miss inside into a single, helping you maintain optimal checkout pathways.

### Can you play darts online with friends using these apps?
Yes. **Target Darts Counter** features dedicated online multiplayer with camera integration, enabling you to play live matches against friends or random players worldwide while viewing their board on screen.

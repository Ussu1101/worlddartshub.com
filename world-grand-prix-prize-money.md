## The Double-In Double-Out Format

The World Grand Prix is famous as the only televised PDC tournament contested entirely under [double-in double-out](/darts/double-in-double-out/) rules.

In standard 501 darts, players can score with any sector on their opening throw. At the World Grand Prix, scoring does not commence until a player lands a dart in a double ring (or the central 50-point bullseye). Any dart landing in a single or treble bed prior to hitting a double scores zero.

To conclude the leg, players must finish on a double or bullseye, exactly as in standard play. This requirement creates severe psychological pressure, as a dry spell at the start of a leg can leave a competitor trailing before their scoring tally has even opened.

### Tournament Sets Structure

Matches at the World Grand Prix are contested in sets, where each set is played as the best of five legs (first to three legs wins the set):

- **Round One (Last 32):** Best of 3 sets (first to 2)
- **Round Two (Last 16):** Best of 5 sets (first to 3)
- **Quarter-Finals:** Best of 5 sets (first to 3)
- **Semi-Finals:** Best of 7 sets (first to 4)
- **Final:** Best of 9 sets (first to 5)

## Executive Summary
The PDC Women’s Series has transformed women’s professional darts into an elite, lucrative global circuit. This guide explains how the 24-event calendar works, how prize funds are distributed across the field, and how the tour provides a direct qualification pipeline to Alexandra Palace, the Winter Gardens, and the Grand Slam of Darts.

---

## 1. What Is the PDC Women’s Series?

Established by the Professional Darts Corporation in 2020, the **PDC Women’s Series** is a dedicated standalone tour designed to provide female darts players with regular, high-standard competitive tournament play. Today, the circuit stands as a cornerstone of the professional darts ecosystem, offering an annual prize fund of £360,000 and providing merit-based routes into the sport's biggest televised major championships.

---

## 2. Eligibility and Entry Requirements

- **Gender & Age Eligibility:** Open to all female players aged 16 and older on the date of the event.
- **Membership:** Entrants must register through the official PDC entry system and hold an active PDPA (Professional Darts Players Association) Associate Membership.
- **Top 64 Restriction:** Any female competitor who holds a full two-year PDC Tour Card and is positioned within the top 64 of the main two-year rolling PDC Order of Merit is ineligible, ensuring the circuit remains focused on open development.
- **Global Participation:** Open to players worldwide.

---

## 3. Tournament Format & Weekend Structure

- **24-Event Annual Structure:** Staged across six multi-event weekends (typically in Milton Keynes, Wigan, Hildesheim, and Leicester).
- **Four Events per Weekend:** Two tournaments on Saturday and two tournaments on Sunday.
- **Leg Format:** Matches up to the Quarter-Finals are best of 7 legs (first to 4); Semi-Finals and Finals are best of 9 legs (first to 5).
- **Draws:** Unseeded open knockout brackets.

## 5. Major Tournament Qualification Pathways

Prize money won translates £-for-£ into points on the official **PDC Women’s Series Order of Merit**:

1. **PDC World Darts Championship (Alexandra Palace):** The top two eligible players on the final Women’s Series Order of Merit qualify directly for Round 1. If an already qualified player occupies a top-two spot, it rolls down to the 3rd-ranked player.
2. **Women’s World Matchplay (Winter Gardens, Blackpool):** The top eight players on a rolling 12-month Women's Series ranking list contest this £40,000 televised major (£15,000 to the winner). The champion earns automatic entry to the Grand Slam and World Championship.
3. **Grand Slam of Darts (Wolverhampton):** Places awarded to the Women’s World Matchplay Champion and the highest-ranked qualifier on the Women’s Series Order of Merit.
4. **PDC Qualifying School (Q-School):** The top eight non-Tour Card holders receive subsidized entries to the Final Stage of Q-School.

## 6. Record Milestones & Historic Champions

- **Beau Greaves:** Record 15 consecutive tournament victories across 2022 and 2023, 70+ match winning streak, and frequent 95+ match averages.
- **Fallon Sherrock:** Historic first woman to win matches at Ally Pally (2019/20), multiple Women's Series titles, and first woman to throw a televised/streamed 9-darter (March 2023).
- **Lisa Ashton:** Four-time World Champion; first woman to win a full Tour Card via open Q-School (2020).
- **Mikuru Suzuki & Noa-Lynn van Leuven:** Prolific international title winners.

## 7. Women's Series vs WDF: Navigating the Circuits

Players must navigate eligibility conflicts between the PDC and WDF. The PDC prohibits players from competing in the PDC World Darts Championship if they choose to play in the concurrent WDF World Championship at Lakeside.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the PDC Women's Series?
The PDC Women's Series is the official professional darts tour for female players, organized by the Professional Darts Corporation. It consists of 24 tournaments held across six weekends each year, offering ranking points that feed directly into the PDC World Darts Championship, Women's World Matchplay, and Grand Slam of Darts.

### How much prize money is on offer in the PDC Women's Series?
Each of the 24 individual Women's Series events carries a £15,000 prize fund (£360,000 across the full season). The winner of each event receives £2,500, the runner-up receives £1,000, and prize money is distributed down to the Last 64.

### How do players qualify for the PDC World Darts Championship from the Women's Series?
The top two players on the final PDC Women's Series Order of Merit gain direct qualification into the first round of the PDC World Darts Championship at Alexandra Palace. If a player in the top two has already qualified via another route (such as winning the Women's World Matchplay), the place rolls down to the next highest-ranked eligible player.

### Who can enter the PDC Women's Series?
The circuit is open to all female dart players aged 16 and over. Competitors must hold PDPA Associate Membership or pay standard event entry fees through the PDC entry system. Female players who hold a standard PDC Tour Card ranked in the world's top 64 are excluded to keep the series focused on open development.

### What is the Women's World Matchplay?
The Women's World Matchplay is an elite eight-player televised major held at the Winter Gardens in Blackpool on the final Sunday of the World Matchplay. It features a £40,000 prize fund (£15,000 to the winner), with qualification determined by a rolling 12-month Women's Series Order of Merit.

### Which players have won the most PDC Women's Series titles?
Beau Greaves is the most dominant champion in Women's Series history, holding the record for the most consecutive titles and highest match averages. Fallon Sherrock, Lisa Ashton, and Mikuru Suzuki have also captured multiple series titles and major qualifications.

# PDC World Darts Championship Tickets 2026/27: Ballot, Prices & Resale Guide

Securing tickets for the PDC World Darts Championship at Alexandra Palace is widely considered one of the most competitive challenges in British sports ticketing. With the historic tournament moving into Alexandra Palace’s Great Hall for the 2026/27 edition—boosting capacity past 5,000 spectators per session—demand remains intensely fierce across all 28 sessions.

This authoritative guide covers official ticketing windows, verified face value pricing, secondary ballot mechanisms, and verified strategies for securing authentic returned tickets after primary allocations sell out.

---

## 1. Official Ticket Sale Windows & The Staggered Ballot

Tickets for the World Darts Championship are distributed through an exclusive partnership between the Professional Darts Corporation (PDC) and official ticketing agent **SeeTickets UK**. Tickets are released in three strict phases:

### Phase 1: PDCTV Annual Subscribers Priority Window (Late July)
Active annual subscribers to PDCTV who hold an active subscription prior to the mid-July qualification deadline receive first access. 
- Subscribers receive a unique, single-use presale access link and passcode.
- Window duration: 48 hours before general sale.
- Allocation: Up to 75% of table seats for premium sessions (opening weekend, post-Christmas, and finals) are claimed during this initial window.

### Phase 2: Registered General Sale (Early August)
Following the subscriber window, any remaining tickets across all sessions open to the general public through SeeTickets.
- Buyers must create and verify a SeeTickets customer account beforehand.
- Peak sessions (Friday evening, Saturday evening, and the New Year quarterfinals/semifinals) routinely sell out within minutes.
- Midweek afternoon sessions in Rounds 1 and 2 offer the highest probability of securing multiple adjacent seats.

### Phase 3: Official SeeTickets Fan-to-Fan Resale (Autumn – January)
The only legally authorized platform to purchase genuine unwanted tickets from fans whose plans have changed.

## 3. Sold Out — Now What? How to Legally Buy Returned Tickets

When standard allocation shows "Sold Out" across the board, genuine tickets frequently return to circulation through official channels as supporters' schedules change. Here are the verified official and practical pathways to secure tickets safely:

### 1. Monitor the Official SeeTickets Fan-to-Fan Resale Platform
The PDC operates a zero-tolerance policy against unauthorized ticket touting. The **SeeTickets Fan-to-Fan Resale Portal** is the single authorized marketplace:
- **Price Capped at Face Value:** Sellers are legally prohibited from charging scalper premiums. Tickets are listed strictly at original face value plus nominal administration fees.
- **Instant Barcode Invalidation:** When a fan purchases a resale ticket, SeeTickets cancels the seller’s digital barcode immediately and issues a completely new, cryptographically unique barcode directly into the buyer’s SeeTickets account.
- **Live Dynamic Availability:** Listings appear dynamically as fans put tickets up for resale. Ticket availability fluctuates and is never guaranteed.

### 2. Fan Advice: Regular Portal Monitoring
Neither the PDC nor SeeTickets operates scheduled release drops or guaranteed batch times. Instead, returns from processed order adjustments, failed payments, or cancellations are fed back into live inventory dynamically. Fans who check the official portal regularly throughout the autumn often discover single seats and pairs as supporters' travel plans shift.

### 3. Broadcast & Staging Technical Holds (Subject to Confirmation)
In early December, once Sky Sports and PDC production teams complete physical stage construction inside Alexandra Palace and finalize camera sightlines, any unallocated technical holds or unused sponsor reserves may be returned to general public sale. Such releases remain occasional and are not guaranteed.

### 4. Official Hospitality Upgrades
When general floor tables and tiered seating are fully booked, official PDC hospitality packages often retain limited allocation:
- Packages include guaranteed prime floor seating near the walkway, three-course dining in the Palm Court, and complimentary beer, wine, and soft drinks.
- While significantly more expensive than standard tickets, they represent a guaranteed, fully authorized route to witness the biggest sessions of the year.

## 4. Anti-Tout Warning: Secondary Resale Blacklist

> **CRITICAL SECURITY WARNING:** Do not buy World Darts Championship tickets from secondary marketplaces such as Viagogo, StubHub, Gigsberg, eBay, or social media sellers (X / Twitter, Facebook groups).

Alexandra Palace utilizes high-throughput optical barcode turnstiles integrated directly with the live SeeTickets central database:
- **Automatic Invalidation:** The PDC’s fraud operations team actively monitors secondary marketplaces, purchases test inventory, and immediately cancels corresponding seat barcodes.
- **Duplicate PDF Scams:** Unscrupulous sellers routinely sell identical PDF printouts to multiple buyers. Only the first individual through the Palm Court turnstiles gains entry; subsequent holders are denied entry by security.
- **No Recourse at Venue:** Alexandra Palace box office staff cannot validate, reprint, or troubleshoot tickets bought outside SeeTickets. Victims of fraud are turned away at the gates without refund.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you get tickets for the PDC World Darts Championship?
Official tickets are sold exclusively via SeeTickets through a staggered priority window. PDCTV Annual Subscribers receive first priority window access (usually late July), followed by registered general public sale. Remaining returned tickets are listed on the official SeeTickets Fan-to-Fan Resale platform.

### How much do World Darts Championship tickets cost at face value?
Face value prices generally range from £40 to £65 for Tiered Bleacher seats, and £50 to £85 for Floor Table seats, with semi-final and final sessions priced between £75 and £120 plus standard booking fees.

### Are tickets bought on Viagogo or StubHub valid for Alexandra Palace?
The PDC and Alexandra Palace strictly void tickets resold on unauthorized secondary platforms like Viagogo, StubHub, or social media. Security scanners at the Palm Court entrance will cancel duplicated or unauthorized barcodes without refund.

### What can I do if PDC World Darts Championship tickets are completely sold out?
If general sales are sold out, monitor the official SeeTickets Fan-to-Fan resale platform where genuine returned tickets are listed at face value, check the portal regularly for fluctuating returns, or explore remaining official hospitality packages.

### When are production hold and sponsor return tickets released?
Unallocated production, broadcast, or sponsor holds are subject to technical stage sign-off in December. Any subsequent releases are occasional and never guaranteed, depending entirely on final camera corridors and venue configurations.

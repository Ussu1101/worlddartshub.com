# BDO World Darts Champions: Every Winner 1978–2020

For more than four decades, the British Darts Organisation (BDO) World Professional Darts Championship stood as the heartbeat of British winter sport. From smoke-filled arenas in the late 1970s to the festive television tradition at the Lakeside Country Club in Frimley Green, the BDO established the legends, rivalries, and vocabulary that defined global darts.

Following the organisation's financial collapse and liquidation in 2020, the BDO championship passed permanently into sporting history. This reference archive provides the complete, verified record of every BDO world champion from Leighton Rees in 1978 to Wayne Warren in 2020.

---

## What Was the BDO?

The British Darts Organisation was founded in 1973 by Olly Croft. In 1978, the BDO launched the inaugural World Professional Darts Championship at the Heart of the Midlands Club in Nottingham, bringing tournament darts to terrestrial network television on the BBC.

The tournament transformed pub games into mainstream sporting entertainment, turning working-class throwers into household sporting icons. For fifteen years, the BDO was the sole world championship in the sport.

However, escalating disputes over television coverage, sponsorship revenue, and tournament promotion led to the seismic 1993 darts split. In January 1993, the top 16 professional players—including Phil Taylor, Eric Bristow, and John Lowe—broke away to establish the World Darts Council (which later became the [Professional Darts Corporation](https://www.pdc.tv/)). 

From 1994 until the BDO's demise in 2020, two parallel world championships were staged each winter. To explore how the two organisations diverged and compare all world titleholders, visit our comprehensive [World Championship Roll of Honour](/history/roll-of-honour/).

## Most BDO World Titles: The Leaderboard

While dozens of competitors reached championship deciders, an elite group dominated the BDO honour roll across multiple eras:

- **Eric Bristow (5 Titles — 1980, 1981, 1984, 1985, 1986):** "The Crafty Cockney" was the defining superstar of darts' golden television age, reaching an astonishing ten BDO finals between 1980 and 1991.
- **[Raymond van Barneveld](/players/raymond-van-barneveld/) (4 Titles — 1998, 1999, 2003, 2005):** "Barney" ignited the Dutch darts explosion with back-to-back Lakeside crowns in the late 1990s before capturing two more titles in 2003 and 2005. He later crossed over to the PDC to claim a fifth world crown in 2007.
- **John Lowe (3 Titles — 1979, 1987, 1993):** "Old Stoneface" achieved the unique distinction of winning world titles across three separate decades (the 1970s, 1980s, and 1990s).
- **Martin Adams (3 Titles — 2007, 2010, 2011):** "Wolfie" served as the talisman of the modern BDO, remaining fiercely loyal to the organisation and lifting three crowns at Lakeside.
- **Glen Durrant (3 Titles — 2017, 2018, 2019):** "Duzza" became only the second player in history to win three consecutive BDO titles, matching Bristow's 1984–1986 hat-trick before moving to the PDC and winning the Premier League in 2020.
- **Two-Time Champions:** Jocky Wilson (1982, 1989), Phil Taylor (1990, 1992), Ted Hankey (2000, 2009), and Scott Waites (2013, 2016).

## The End of the BDO

The demise of the British Darts Organisation was precipitated by years of commercial mismanagement, dwindling television audiences, and the departure of marquee names to the PDC circuit.

The breaking point arrived in 2019 and 2020. After leaving its spiritual home at Lakeside to stage the 2020 World Championship at Indigo at The O2 in London, ticket sales collapsed and commercial sponsorship failed to materialize. Days before the 2020 championship commenced, prize money was drastically slashed across all rounds.

Wales' Wayne Warren captured the final BDO world title on 12 January 2020, defeating compatriot Jim Williams 7–4. At 57 years and 219 days, Warren became the oldest world champion in darts history. However, due to the organisation's financial insolvency, Warren received just £23,000 of the advertised £100,000 champion's prize fund.

In September 2020, the British Darts Organisation officially entered liquidation with debts exceeding £700,000, bringing an unceremonious conclusion to a 47-year institutional history. Today, global professional darts is unified under the PDC, while grassroots and amateur competitions continue through the World Darts Federation (WDF) and the [Professional Darts Players Association](https://pdpa.co.uk/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who has won the most BDO world titles?
Eric Bristow won the most BDO World Professional Darts Championships, capturing five titles in 1980, 1981, 1984, 1985, and 1986.

### Who was the last BDO world champion?
Wayne Warren of Wales was the final BDO world champion, winning the 2020 championship at Indigo at The O2 by defeating Jim Williams 7–4 on 12 January 2020.

### What happened to the BDO?
The British Darts Organisation collapsed in 2020 following decades of commercial decline, falling TV revenues, and severe financial insolvency. After drastically cutting player prize money at the 2020 World Championship, the company went into formal liquidation in September 2020.

### Did Phil Taylor play in the BDO?
Yes. Phil Taylor began his professional career in the BDO under the mentorship of Eric Bristow, winning two BDO World Championships in 1990 and 1992 before leading the breakaway to form the PDC in 1993.

### Where was the BDO World Championship held?
The tournament was held at the Heart of the Midlands Club in Nottingham (1978), the Jollees Club in Stoke-on-Trent (1979–1985), the Lakeside Country Club in Frimley Green (1986–2019), and Indigo at The O2 in London for its final 2020 edition.

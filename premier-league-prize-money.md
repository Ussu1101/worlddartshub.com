# Premier League Darts Prize Money: Full Breakdown & Nightly Bonuses

The Premier League Darts is the sport's biggest, most lucrative weekly roadshow. Staged across 17 consecutive Thursday nights in premier arenas throughout the UK, Ireland, Germany, and the Netherlands, the tournament boasts a total prize purse of **£1,250,000**.

Unlike traditional weekend ranking tournaments, the Premier League features an intense, multi-tiered reward structure that combines massive end-of-season Play-Off prizes with high-stakes **£10,000 nightly knockout bonuses**.

This complete financial guide provides the authoritative breakdown of the £350,000 champion’s payout, weekly bonuses, nine-dart incentives, and the crucial regulatory distinction regarding the PDC Order of Merit.

---

## 1. At A Glance: Premier League Purse Highlights

- **Total Prize Fund:** £1,250,000
- **Champion’s Cheque:** £350,000 (Awarded to the Play-Offs winner at The O2 in London)
- **Runner-Up Payout:** £170,000
- **Semi-Finalists:** £110,000 each (Two players eliminated in the Play-Offs)
- **Nightly Knockout Bonuses:** £160,000 total (£10,000 cash bonus awarded to each of the 16 weekly night champions)
- **Order of Merit Status:** £0 (Unranked invitational tournament)

## 3. The £10,000 Nightly Knockout Bonus System

In 2022, the PDC abandoned the legacy round-robin format in favor of a high-octane nightly knockout bracket. Every Thursday night now operates as an independent mini-tournament:

1. **Quarter-Finals (4 Matches):** Best of 11 legs. The four winners earn 2 league points.
2. **Semi-Finals (2 Matches):** Best of 11 legs. The two winners advance and secure 3 league points.
3. **The Night Final (1 Match):** The victor captures **5 league table points** and an immediate **£10,000 cash cheque**.

This nightly cash incentive ensures that dead rubbers are impossible. Even if a player is mathematically eliminated from Play-Off contention by Week 13, every Thursday night offers £10,000 in immediate performance bonus money.

## 4. Why Premier League Prize Money Does NOT Count for the Order of Merit

A common question among darts followers is why Premier League prize earnings do not reflect on the official **PDC Order of Merit**.

Under Darts Regulation Authority (DRA) and PDC rules:
- The **PDC Order of Merit** is a two-year rolling ranking table open to all 128 Tour Card holders through open qualification and ProTour events.
- The **Premier League** is strictly an unranked, eight-player invitational tournament (typically featuring the top four players from the PDC Order of Merit plus four invited wildcards).
- Crediting £1,250,000 into the two-year world ranking table would distort the meritocratic hierarchy, granting invited players an unassailable financial buffer over the rest of the professional tour.

## 5. Nine-Dart Finish Incentives in the Premier League

The cauldron atmosphere of Premier League arenas has generated iconic nine-dart moments: Phil Taylor’s double nine-darters in the 2010 final, Adrian Lewis in Belfast, and Gerwyn Price's extraordinary two nine-darters in a single night in Belfast in 2022.

While the PDC does not operate a rolling jackpot for nine-darters in the Premier League, title sponsors traditionally award bespoke commemorative tokens—such as engraved 18-carat solid gold darts pins—alongside charitable donations made in the player's name.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### How much prize money does the Premier League Darts winner receive?
The Premier League Darts champion receives a guaranteed top prize of £350,000 for winning the Play-Offs final at London's O2 Arena, plus any accumulated £10,000 weekly night bonuses earned throughout the 16 regular league rounds.

### Does Premier League Darts prize money count towards the PDC Order of Merit?
No. The Premier League is strictly an unranked invitational tournament. Under official PDC rules, money won in the Premier League does not count towards the two-year rolling PDC Order of Merit world rankings.

### How does the £10,000 nightly winner bonus work?
Across each of the 16 regular league nights, an 8-player bracket is contested. The player who wins the final on the night receives an immediate £10,000 winner bonus in addition to 5 league table points. Runners-up and semifinalists receive league points but no nightly cash bonus.

### How much do players finishing 5th through 8th receive?
Players failing to qualify for the Play-Offs still receive lucrative prize money: 5th place earns £95,000, 6th place receives £90,000, 7th place receives £85,000, and 8th place receives £80,000.

### Is there a bonus for hitting a nine-dart finish in the Premier League?
While there is no standard PDC rolling prize fund for nine-darters, sponsor partners traditionally award commemorative prizes—such as custom 18-carat solid gold darts or partner charity donations—for a perfect leg hit live on broadcast.

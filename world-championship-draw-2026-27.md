# World Darts Championship 2026/27 Draw Guide: 128-Player Bracket

The **2026/27 PDC World Darts Championship** marks the most monumental expansion in professional darts history. For the first time at Alexandra Palace, the tournament field has expanded from 96 to **128 competitors**, shifting into the majestic Great Hall under a £5,000,000 total prize fund.

This structural expansion fundamentally alters the nature of the draw. Under previous 96-player formats, the top 32 seeds enjoyed an automatic bye into Round 2. In 2026/27, **byes have been completely abolished**. Every player—from the reigning world champion to international debutants—must fight through Round 1 across 64 explosive opening ties.

Here is the authoritative timed guide to the official draw ceremony, seeding cutoffs, television broadcasts, and the structure of the four bracket quarters.

---

## 1. Official Draw Timeline & Broadcast Details

The official tournament draw is synchronized with the conclusion of the PDC ProTour season:

- **Seeding & Qualification Cutoff:** Late November 2026, immediately following the final match of the Players Championship Finals in Minehead.
- **Official Draw Ceremony:** Held on the Monday following the Minehead finals (typically broadcast live on Monday afternoon, with exact broadcast timing pending official PDC and Sky Sports programming confirmation).
- **Television Coverage:** Broadcast live in high definition on **Sky Sports News** across the UK and Ireland.
- **Global Digital Stream:** Streamed live worldwide free of charge via the **official PDC YouTube Channel** and **PDCTV**.
- **Daily Match Schedule Release:** The confirmed day-by-day running order (dividing matches across 28 afternoon and evening sessions) is released within 48 to 72 hours of the draw.

## 2. The Format Revolution: 64 Round 1 Matches, No Byes

In every World Championship between 2006 and 2025, the top 32 ranked players in the PDC Order of Merit sat out the opening round. 

Under the new 128-player single-elimination framework:
1. **Zero Byes:** Seeds 1 through 32 are placed directly into 32 distinct seeded positions in Round 1.
2. **64 Opening Matches:** All 128 contenders contest Round 1 (Best of 5 Sets) across the opening nine days of competition.
3. **Seeded vs. Unseeded:** Each of the top 32 seeds is drawn against an unseeded qualifier, drawn from the ProTour Order of Merit qualifiers, Challenge/Development Tour representatives, and international champions.
4. **Instant Jeopardy:** Elite stars no longer have the luxury of a practice week to adjust to stage conditions; they face elimination from Day 1.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### When is the official draw for the 2026/27 PDC World Darts Championship?
The official 128-player bracket draw takes place in late November 2026, immediately following the conclusion of the Players Championship Finals in Minehead, which serves as the final Order of Merit seeding cutoff.

### Do the top 32 seeds receive a bye to Round 2 in the 128-player format?
No. Under the expanded 128-player format, the traditional Round 2 bye for top 32 seeds has been eliminated. All 128 competitors—including the world's top seeds—must play in Round 1 across 64 unmissable opening matches.

### Where can fans watch the 2026/27 World Championship draw live?
The draw ceremony will be broadcast live across Sky Sports News in the UK and streamed simultaneously worldwide on the official PDC YouTube channel and PDCTV.

### How are the four bracket quarters structured?
The 128-player bracket is divided into four distinct quarters of 32 players each. World No. 1 headlines Quarter 1, World No. 2 anchors Quarter 3, World No. 3 anchors Quarter 4, and World No. 4 heads Quarter 2, ensuring top-four seeds cannot meet until the semifinals.

### When will specific session schedules and player match times be confirmed?
